Common questions

Simple answers for therapists and families.

Does NAVIA diagnose autism?

No. NAVIA is designed as a clinician-support and progress-tracking platform. Diagnosis should remain with appropriately qualified professionals using validated clinical procedures.

Can parents use NAVIA without a therapist?

The strongest use case is collaborative: therapists define goals and review materials, while parents support practice and share observations from everyday routines.

What kinds of communication can be tracked?

Examples include expressive and receptive language, speech intelligibility, AAC use, requesting, turn-taking, joint attention, conversation, and other individualized functional communication goals.

How are AI-generated stories and scripts used?

They are starting points for therapist review. The therapist can edit language, visuals, complexity, prompts, and context before sharing them with a family.
