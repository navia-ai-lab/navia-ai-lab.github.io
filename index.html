Multimodal AI research for autism progress support.

ASD screening research, progress tracking, and personalized communication support.

NAVIA is a multidisciplinary research initiative developing multimodal AI to study ASD screening signals and use them longitudinally for progress tracking. The platform helps therapists monitor voice and communication features and create personalized supports such as social stories, images, and child-centred practice activities.

Research pipeline

From ASD screening signals to therapy progress tracking.

NAVIA studies multimodal markers of ASD and explores how those signals can help therapists track communication progress and personalize intervention supports over time.

1

Multimodal data

Speech, voice, language, interaction, and behavioral cues gathered through culturally grounded research protocols.

2

ASD screening signals

Models examine multimodal markers associated with ASD and surface interpretable patterns for therapist review.

3

Progress baseline

Screening-related signals become a structured baseline for tracking voice, language, and communication change over time.

4

Personalized supports

Communication profiles guide AI-assisted stories, images, scripts, and practice activities tailored to each autistic child.

5

Therapist monitoring

Therapists review progress trends across sessions, voice features, generated materials, and caregiver-supported practice.

NAVIA platform

A therapist-facing AI platform for screening-informed progress support.

The NAVIA prototype connects ASD screening research with voice-feature tracking, social-story generation, and clinician-guided communication support for autistic children.

Screening-informed tracking

Use ASD screening-related markers as a research baseline for monitoring change across therapy sessions and practice routines.

Voice-feature analysis

Track voice and speech features such as vocalization, pronunciation patterns, clarity, rhythm, and communication attempts.

Social story generation

Help therapists create personalized social stories with child-centred text, images, scripts, and visual prompts.

Communication support

Recommend individualized speech, language, and interaction activities based on each child's profile and progress.

Generated support examples

Therapists can use NAVIA to draft image-supported social stories, communication scripts, pronunciation and vocalization practice, imitation tasks, joint-attention games, role-play prompts, and progress notes for review.

For clinicians

A research prototype for therapists tracking voice, communication, and progress.

NAVIA is being developed to help therapists review ASD screening-informed signals, track voice and communication features over time, and turn those insights into personalized communication supports for autistic children.

  • Review ASD screening markers as progress-tracking baselines
  • Track voice features, pronunciation, vocalization, and speech patterns
  • Generate social stories with text, images, scripts, and visual prompts
  • Use communication profiles to personalize intervention activities
  • Monitor progress with session notes and caregiver-supported practice data

Responsible AI

Human-guided AI for screening-informed autism support.

NAVIA recognizes autistic speech and behavioral patterns as meaningful communication. The research prototype is designed to help therapists track progress and generate supports, not replace clinical assessment or professional judgment.

Clinical oversight

Therapists and qualified clinicians remain responsible for interpretation, assessment, and intervention decisions.

Cultural sensitivity

Arabic-English support and Qatar-based research context help ground generated stories, images, and activities in local language and care needs.

Data responsibility

Multimodal research data should be collected with consent-aware workflows, privacy safeguards, and clear access controls.

Transparent AI

Voice-feature trends, progress summaries, and generated stories or images should be explainable, reviewable, and editable before use.
