Research Behind NAVIA

From multimodal communication signals to adaptive support

NAVIA investigates how speech, voice, language, interaction, and behavioral signals can provide clinically meaningful measures of autistic communication and change over time. We study how these multimodal signals can support ASD assessment, longitudinal progress monitoring, and therapist-guided personalized intervention across Arabic and English contexts.

Multimodal Communication Markers Speech · Voice · Language · Interaction · Behavior
Longitudinal Progress Modeling Change over time, not one-time scores
