About NAVIA

Neurodiversity Assessment and Voice-enabled Intervention AI

NAVIA is a multidisciplinary research project exploring AI-assisted ASD progress tracking, longitudinal communication tracking, and personalized speech and language support for autistic children. The project brings together AI research, autism expertise, speech-language practice, behavioral support, ethics, and family participation.

Research behind NAVIA

Multimodal AI for ASD progress tracking and personalized support.

NAVIA studies whether speech, language, interaction, and behavioral markers can support ASD screening research. We also study how these signals can be used longitudinally to track communication progress and guide personalized support.

The research emphasizes culturally and linguistically appropriate AI, including Arabic and English, with clinicians and families in the loop.

Responsible AI

AI supports the therapist. It does not replace the therapist.

NAVIA is designed around human oversight, family participation, privacy, and respect for autistic communication.

Therapist-led

Professionals review AI-generated insights and materials before they guide support.

Family-centred

Parents and caregivers contribute everyday context and support practice outside therapy.

Privacy-aware

Sensitive voice, video, and behavioral data require consent-aware and access-controlled workflows.

Transparent

AI-generated summaries, stories, images, and recommendations should remain reviewable and editable.
