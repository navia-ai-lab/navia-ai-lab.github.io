NAVIA platform

Four research pillars for autism screening and communication support.

The NAVIA prototype connects multimodal AI analysis with clinician-guided intervention planning for autistic children.

Multimodal screening

Analyze speech, language, visual, and behavioral cues to support early ASD screening and clinician review.

Communication profile

Characterize speech, language, interaction, and communication patterns to identify individual strengths and needs.

Personalized intervention

Support clinicians in creating individualized speech and language activities appropriate to each learner's profile.

Progress monitoring

Track communication progress across therapy sessions and caregiver-supported practice over time.

Intervention examples

Personalized supports may include pronunciation practice, sound vocalization, imitation tasks, joint-attention games, conversation scripts, role-play, social stories, and real-time feedback activities.

Responsible AI

Culturally grounded, human-guided AI for autism research.

NAVIA recognizes autistic speech and behavioral patterns as meaningful communication. The research prototype is designed to support qualified professionals, not replace clinical assessment or professional judgment.

Clinical oversight

Clinicians remain responsible for interpretation, assessment, and intervention decisions.

Cultural sensitivity

Arabic-English support and Qatar-based research context help ground NAVIA in local language and care needs.

Data responsibility

Multimodal research data should be collected with consent-aware workflows, privacy safeguards, and clear access controls.

Transparent AI

Screening outputs, reports, and intervention suggestions should be explainable, reviewable, and editable before use.
